Privacy & analytics

Updated September 17, 2026

Your choice

We use optional Google Analytics to understand visits and which parts of the website people find useful. Google Analytics loads only after you choose “Allow analytics.” Declining does not affect the worksheet or other site features. Change your choice using Analytics preferences below or in the website footer.

What analytics measures

When allowed, Google Analytics receives information about public pages visited, traffic sources, browser/device characteristics, and actions such as selecting a case study, completing the worksheet, downloading or copying a brief, or clicking an email or LinkedIn link. An email click does not tell us whether a message was sent.

Google receives connection information, including your IP address, when your browser contacts its service. Analytics cookies identify a browser across visits; they do not tell us who you are. We disable Google signals and advertising personalization in our website configuration. Microsoft Clarity and session recording are not installed.

Our analytics events do not include worksheet answers, generated briefs, email-link contents, or arbitrary URL query strings. Simple campaign labels may be used to identify the source of a visit. The unlisted review draft is not instrumented, and the privacy page itself is not tracked.

Cookies and preferences

Your analytics choice is stored in this browser for up to 180 days. If allowed, Google Analytics cookies (_ga and _ga_7DQS782PJN) are configured for up to 180 days. Choosing “No thanks” stops further analytics collection on this site and removes these accessible cookies. It does not erase data already collected by Google. Browser storage restrictions or clearing your browser data may cause us to ask again.

We also honor the browser’s Global Privacy Control signal by leaving analytics off. Your choice applies to this browser, not automatically to your other devices.

The task worksheet and email

The worksheet runs in your browser. Its answers and generated brief are not submitted to our server or Google Analytics. Copy and Download let you save or share them yourself. “Discuss this with Joe” opens an email draft containing the brief; you decide whether to send it. If you email us, we receive and use your message to respond to your inquiry.

Hosting and providers

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process connection and request information to provide hosting. See GitHub’s privacy statement. For Google’s handling of analytics data, see Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner sites.
