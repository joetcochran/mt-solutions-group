Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Virtastack, operated by Joe Cochran (“Virtastack,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you visit virtastack.com, contact us, submit a form on our website, or submit a Virtastack Lead Gen Form on LinkedIn.

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, company, job information, and the details of your question, project, or workflow. The exact fields depend on the form you use.

If you submit a Virtastack Lead Gen Form on LinkedIn, LinkedIn provides us with the information shown on that form after you submit it. This may include information populated from your LinkedIn profile and any answers you enter. LinkedIn also processes information about your use of its services under LinkedIn’s Privacy Policy.

When you visit our website, our hosting provider receives technical request information needed to deliver the site. If you allow optional analytics, we and our analytics providers also collect the website-usage information described below.

How we use personal information

We use information you submit to:

  • respond to your request, question, or consultation inquiry;
  • contact you about the Virtastack services or resource identified on the form;
  • prepare for and provide services you ask us to perform;
  • maintain our business records, protect the site, prevent misuse, and comply with legal obligations; and
  • send marketing communications only when permitted. You may ask us to stop at any time.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

How we share personal information

We share personal information only as needed with service providers that help us operate the website, deliver forms and email, manage communications, or provide requested services; when you direct or authorize us to share it; or when required to comply with law, protect rights and safety, or complete a business transfer. Providers may process information under their own privacy terms and in the countries where they operate.

LinkedIn collects and sends Lead Gen Form information through its platform. Our website form provider, Web3Forms, forwards website submissions to Joe’s inbox. We do not disclose form answers or contact details to Google Analytics, Google Ads, or Microsoft Clarity as analytics event content.

Retention and your choices

We keep inquiry and customer information only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain the relationship, provide requested services, meet legal or recordkeeping requirements, and resolve disputes. Retention therefore depends on the nature of the interaction. Service providers may retain information according to their own policies.

You may contact us to ask what personal information we hold about you or to request correction or deletion. We will honor applicable rights and legal requirements. You may also opt out of marketing email at any time by replying or contacting us. These choices do not prevent essential replies about an active request or service.

Optional website analytics

We use optional Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand visits and which parts of the website people find useful. Both tools load only after you choose “Allow analytics.” Declining does not affect the get-started forms or other site features. Change your choice using Analytics preferences below or in the website footer.

What analytics measures

When allowed, Google Analytics receives information about public pages visited, traffic sources, browser/device characteristics, and actions such as selecting a case study or clicking an email or LinkedIn link. An email click does not tell us whether a message was sent. After Web3Forms confirms a successful consultation or guided-questionnaire inquiry, Google Ads receives a conversion event with no form answers or contact details.

Google and Microsoft receive connection information, including your IP address, when your browser contacts their services. Analytics cookies identify a browser across visits; they do not tell us who you are. We disable Google signals and advertising personalization in our website configuration. Microsoft Clarity provides heatmaps and session replays of page interactions. The get-started page's consultation, AI Coach, and questionnaire panels, including your contact details, answers, and generated brief, are explicitly masked from Clarity replay. We send Clarity analytics consent but deny advertising storage consent.

Our analytics events do not include your contact details, answers, generated briefs, email-link contents, or arbitrary URL query strings in custom events. When analytics is allowed, Clarity may process recognized advertising click identifiers and simple campaign labels in a landing-page URL; visits containing any other query parameter, an unrecognized fragment, or a query-bearing/private internal referrer are excluded from Clarity. Unlisted drafts are not instrumented, and the privacy page itself is not tracked.

Cookies and preferences

Your analytics choice is stored in this browser for up to 180 days. If allowed, Google Analytics cookies (_ga and _ga_7DQS782PJN) are configured for up to 180 days. Clarity also uses first-party cookies such as _clck (up to one year) and _clsk (up to one day). Choosing “No thanks” stops further analytics collection on this site and removes accessible Google Analytics and Clarity cookies. It does not erase data already collected by the providers. Browser storage restrictions or clearing your browser data may cause us to ask again.

We also honor the browser’s Global Privacy Control signal by leaving analytics off. Your choice applies to this browser, not automatically to your other devices.

The get-started page and email

The consultation form, AI Coach, and questionnaire on the get-started page run in your browser. When you send your contact details and answers, they are submitted directly to Web3Forms, a third-party form-delivery service, which forwards them to Joe's inbox; we don't store them on a server of our own, and they are never sent as analytics event content. If that direct submission fails for any reason, your browser opens an email draft containing the same information instead—nothing is sent until you send it yourself. The questionnaire also lets you copy or download its brief as plain text.

Hosting and providers

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process connection and request information to provide hosting. See GitHub’s privacy statement. For Google’s handling of analytics data, see Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner sites.

For Microsoft’s handling of Clarity data, see Microsoft’s privacy statement. Masking protects the get-started page's text fields, but interaction information such as clicks and scrolling is still recorded when allowed.

For Web3Forms's handling of get-started page submissions, see Web3Forms's privacy policy.

Security, children, and policy changes

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to a small consulting business to protect personal information. No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure. Our services are intended for businesses and adults, not children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy as our practices or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top shows the latest revision.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact Virtastack operator Joe Cochran at joe@virtastack.com.
