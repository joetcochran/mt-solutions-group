Privacy & analytics

Updated September 25, 2026

Your choice

We use optional Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand visits and which parts of the website people find useful. Both tools load only after you choose “Allow analytics.” Declining does not affect the get-started forms or other site features. Change your choice using Analytics preferences below or in the website footer.

What analytics measures

When allowed, Google Analytics receives information about public pages visited, traffic sources, browser/device characteristics, and actions such as selecting a case study or clicking an email or LinkedIn link. An email click does not tell us whether a message was sent.

Google and Microsoft receive connection information, including your IP address, when your browser contacts their services. Analytics cookies identify a browser across visits; they do not tell us who you are. We disable Google signals and advertising personalization in our website configuration. Microsoft Clarity provides heatmaps and session replays of page interactions. The get-started page's AI Coach and questionnaire panels, including your answers and the generated brief, are explicitly masked from Clarity replay. We send Clarity analytics consent but deny advertising storage consent.

Our analytics events do not include your answers, generated briefs, email-link contents, or arbitrary URL query strings in custom events. Clarity is not loaded on visits with URL query strings, unrecognized fragments, or query-bearing/private internal referrers. Simple campaign labels may be used to identify the source of a visit. Unlisted drafts are not instrumented, and the privacy page itself is not tracked.

Cookies and preferences

Your analytics choice is stored in this browser for up to 180 days. If allowed, Google Analytics cookies (_ga and _ga_7DQS782PJN) are configured for up to 180 days. Clarity also uses first-party cookies such as _clck (up to one year) and _clsk (up to one day). Choosing “No thanks” stops further analytics collection on this site and removes accessible Google Analytics and Clarity cookies. It does not erase data already collected by the providers. Browser storage restrictions or clearing your browser data may cause us to ask again.

We also honor the browser’s Global Privacy Control signal by leaving analytics off. Your choice applies to this browser, not automatically to your other devices.

The get-started page and email

The AI Coach and questionnaire on the get-started page run in your browser. When you send your answers, they are submitted directly to Web3Forms, a third-party form-delivery service, which forwards them to Joe's inbox; we don't store them on a server of our own, and they are never sent as analytics event content. If that direct submission fails for any reason, your browser opens an email draft containing the same brief instead—nothing is sent until you send it yourself. Either way, Copy and Download let you save or share the brief on your own. If a brief or email reaches Joe, he uses it only to respond to your inquiry.

Hosting and providers

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process connection and request information to provide hosting. See GitHub’s privacy statement. For Google’s handling of analytics data, see Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner sites.

For Microsoft’s handling of Clarity data, see Microsoft’s privacy statement. Masking protects the get-started page's text fields, but interaction information such as clicks and scrolling is still recorded when allowed.

For Web3Forms's handling of get-started page submissions, see Web3Forms's privacy policy.
